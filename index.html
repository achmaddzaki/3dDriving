Welcome to Zack's Vibe Coding Challenge

This project is a 3D driving simulation of field sales work using the Simplidots app. You drive a vehicle through a town, visit customers and the distributor, and complete the task assigned to your user type.

How to play

  1. Choose a vehicle and a user type from the menu (Esc or the Menu button).
  2. Follow the yellow marker and the arrow above your car to reach the next target.
  3. Park at a store. When the car is nearly still, the Simplidots app slides out on the right.
  4. Check in, then follow the steps shown in the app, such as creating orders, taking pictures, or checking out.
  5. Drive with W/A/S/D or the arrow keys, use Space to brake hard, and press R to reset the car to the distributor.

About this project

No manual coding was applied during the entire build process. The whole project was created through AI prompting, as a vibe coding experiment.
